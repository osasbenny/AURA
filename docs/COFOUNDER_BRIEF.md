# AURA — CO-FOUNDER BRIEF V2

**Confidential working document**  
**September 2026**

## 1. Why This Document Exists

AURA is no longer being considered only as a software project.

The opportunity is to build a real company around a difficult problem: making fundraising easier while making giving safer and more trustworthy.

This brief is for a potential co-founder or early leadership partner. It explains the company, opportunity, operating model and questions that must be resolved before equity or money changes hands.

## 2. The One-Line Idea

**AURA helps people raise money for real needs by simply having a conversation.**

## 3. The Vision

**Human generosity, amplified by AI.**

The long-term ambition is to build infrastructure that allows individuals, families, communities and organizations across Africa to raise, receive and track support more easily — and eventually connect African causes with donors anywhere in the world.

## 4. Why AURA Exists

People already help each other. The missing layer is infrastructure.

Today, someone may have a genuine emergency but still struggle with writing a campaign, presenting evidence, reaching donors, establishing credibility, handling payments, answering donor questions, providing updates, tracking money and dealing with support issues.

AURA combines these pieces into one system.

## 5. What Makes AURA Different

### WhatsApp-first
A person should not have to learn a complicated fundraising platform before asking for help.

### AI-native
AI does more than generate copy. It acts as the conversational operating layer across campaign creation, support, updates and navigation.

### Trust-native
Verification is not an afterthought. AURA separates identity, claim, evidence and financial settlement.

### Human-in-the-loop
Some situations require judgment, empathy and investigation. AURA combines AI with human trust/support operations.

### Africa-first
The product is designed around African realities: WhatsApp adoption, local payment infrastructure, local communication patterns, diaspora relationships and fragmented institutional evidence.

## 6. Typical User Experience

A fundraiser sends AURA a WhatsApp message:

> “I need help raising money for my mother's surgery.”

AURA asks questions, collects the story, identifies required evidence, helps create the campaign and explains verification.

The campaign goes through the appropriate trust process. Once approved, the fundraiser receives a campaign link.

Donors can discover the campaign, understand what is being raised, review trust signals, donate through approved payment infrastructure and receive updates.

The fundraiser can continue managing the campaign through WhatsApp and the web.

If something becomes sensitive or complicated, a human support/trust agent can take over.

## 7. The Four Operating Pillars

### AURA AI
Conversational interface and intelligence layer.

### AURA Trust
Identity, evidence, risk, verification and fraud/abuse operations.

### AURA Payments
Approved payment infrastructure, reconciliation, refunds and payouts.

### AURA Human
Support, trust operations, partnerships and relationship management.

Technology is the infrastructure underneath all four.

## 8. The Trust Question

One of the biggest questions is:

**How do we verify a fundraising claim when Nigeria does not have a single central database containing every hospital, school or institutional record?**

The answer is not “AI will know.”

AURA uses layered evidence.

### Evidence
Medical reports, invoices, prescriptions, admission documents, school documents, photographs and other relevant evidence.

### Evidence Intelligence
OCR, metadata, duplicates, image consistency, manipulation signals, geographic consistency, claim consistency and other risk signals.

Metadata is evidence context, not proof.

### Identity
An approved identity provider such as Dojah can help establish who the fundraiser is. But identity verification does not prove the story.

### Human Review
High-risk or uncertain cases go to people.

This creates a **human-in-the-loop trust system**, not an “AI decides who is a fraudster” system.

## 9. The Legal Question

AURA must not simply start accepting and paying out money because a payment provider has an API.

The company needs professional Nigerian fintech/regulatory legal advice.

The lawyer must determine:

- what AURA legally is
- whether donation crowdfunding creates licensing obligations
- whether AURA ever possesses customer funds
- how payment providers and connected accounts should be structured
- how payouts work
- how refunds and chargebacks work
- what CBN/SEC rules may apply
- what data-protection obligations apply
- how medical/identity evidence should be stored and retained

Strategic principle:

**AURA should minimize or avoid direct custody of customer funds unless a lawyer and approved regulatory/payment structure explicitly permit it.**

## 10. Why Human Support Matters

AURA should be AI-first, not AI-only.

Some users will simply prefer to speak with a human.

Human support can answer questions, help users who struggle with AI, handle sensitive cases, investigate discrepancies, support donors, handle appeals, escalate financial/trust issues and maintain the human character of the company.

The ideal experience:

**AI gets you most of the way there. A human is always available when the situation deserves one.**

## 12. What Must Be Agreed Before Equity

Do not start with:

> “How much percentage do you get?”

Start with:

> “What company are we building, what does each founder contribute, and what happens if circumstances change?”

A founders agreement should cover roles, responsibilities, equity, vesting, decision rights, founder contributions, expenses, salaries, IP ownership, confidentiality, departure, underperformance, future fundraising, dilution, deadlock, sale, failure and dispute resolution.

Equity should reflect durable contribution, responsibility and risk — not simply who had the original idea.

## 13. Early Funding

A serious founder/beta phase may require approximately:

**₦2m–₦4.1m**

A practical planning target is approximately:

**₦3m**

The money is not primarily for software development because the technical founder can build much of the initial system. It is for making the company real: legal/regulatory work, company setup, payment-provider work, verification/testing, cloud/software, WhatsApp/communications, human support, branding, early marketing and contingency.

## 14. Founder Sweat Equity

The technical work includes frontend engineering, backend engineering, database design, AI orchestration, WhatsApp integration, cloud infrastructure, security, testing, DevOps and admin tooling.

This can represent a multi-million-naira external development cost.

Founder contribution should be documented transparently rather than treated as an invisible free resource.

## 16. Initial Market

Start in Nigeria.

Focus initially on medical, emergency, education and family/community campaigns.

Then:

**Nigeria → Africa → African diaspora → global giving infrastructure**

## 17. First-Stage Success

We do not need millions of users immediately. We need evidence that the machine works.

Success means we can reliably:

1. onboard a real fundraiser
2. create a campaign through conversation
3. collect evidence
4. verify identity
5. review trust
6. publish an approved campaign
7. receive a real donation through approved infrastructure
8. reconcile it correctly
9. notify the fundraiser
10. provide support
11. publish updates
12. complete the campaign safely

## 18. The Bigger Company

AURA can eventually become more than a consumer crowdfunding website.

Potential future products include NGO fundraising infrastructure, enterprise fundraising, white-label fundraising, fundraising APIs, donor relationship management, verification infrastructure, impact reporting, cross-border giving infrastructure and institutional partnerships.

The consumer product is the wedge.

The long-term opportunity is **fundraising infrastructure**.

## 19. What We Should Not Build Yet

Do not let ambition destroy focus.

MVP should not become an investment platform, lending platform, crypto platform, social network, internal wallet, blockchain project, native mobile app or everything-for-everyone charity ecosystem.

The first objective is to make one core loop excellent.

## 20. The Core Question for a Co-Founder

The question is not:

**“Do you like the idea?”**

It is:

**“Are we willing to build the company required to make this idea trustworthy, useful and sustainable?”**

That means being prepared to work on policies, support, legal, partnerships, trust reviews, operations, customer complaints, reconciliation, documentation, fundraising and market testing.

The software is only one part of AURA.

## 21. Founding Principle

**Technology should make generosity easier — without making trust weaker.**

Operating principle:

**AI should remove friction; systems should create accountability; humans should handle judgment.**
